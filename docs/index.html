About

Peace Soldiers is building a human-centered network where real missions connect to transparent funding and measurable outcomes.

What we do

MVP
We start with a simple core: Peace Pass (identity), missions (action), and a clear impact signal (HII). The MVP focuses on a clean flow inside Telegram, with documents and progress updates accessible from day one.

How trust is built

Verification
Missions are designed with scope, points, and review rules. Partner organizations can validate field activity, and reports can be logged in a consistent format. The goal is to keep impact auditable and useful, not performative.

What happens next

Phases
Later phases add wallet, rewards, governance access, and partner benefits. Each step ships with public documentation and clear constraints. Progress is shared through the News page and the monthly newsletter.
